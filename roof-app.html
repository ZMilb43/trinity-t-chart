Laminated architectural shingles with a 1¼-inch ArmourZone nailing band, FastLock sun-activated sealant, Class 3 impact rating, and a limited wind warranty up to 130 mph. Limited lifetime coverage with 15-year Iron Clad protection, plus blue-green algae resistance. Three bundles cover a true square.
